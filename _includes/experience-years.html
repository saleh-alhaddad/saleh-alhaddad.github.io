{%- comment -%} Whole years of experience since site.data.author.career_start, computed at BUILD time. Emits a bare integer (e.g. "8"); the "+" is written in the markup, not here. This is only the no-JavaScript fallback: assets/js/modern.js recomputes every .calc-years element on load, so the figure stays right between site builds. Usage: {% include experience-years.html %}+ years {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _start = site.data.author.career_start -%} {%- assign _sy = _start | date: "%Y" | times: 1 -%} {%- assign _sm = _start | date: "%m" | times: 1 -%} {%- assign _sd = _start | date: "%d" | times: 1 -%} {%- assign _ny = site.time | date: "%Y" | times: 1 -%} {%- assign _nm = site.time | date: "%m" | times: 1 -%} {%- assign _nd = site.time | date: "%d" | times: 1 -%} {%- assign _years = _ny | minus: _sy -%} {%- assign _months = _nm | minus: _sm -%} {%- if _nd < _sd -%}{%- assign _months = _months | minus: 1 -%}{%- endif -%} {%- if _months < 0 -%}{%- assign _years = _years | minus: 1 -%}{%- endif -%} {{- _years -}}
